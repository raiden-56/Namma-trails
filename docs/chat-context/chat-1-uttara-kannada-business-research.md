## Request 1: tourism business

**What was asked:** which digital business related to tourism can earn revenue in Uttara Kannada. Do research, find the gap and the high-revenue opportunity.

**Short answer:** the biggest gap is on the supply side. Tourists already come, but most homestays and activity operators are small, unorganised and hard to book online. The best business is one that organises that supply and sells it as bookable stays and packaged trips.

### What the research showed

- **Demand is large.** About 1.03 crore visits in 2022, led by Gokarna and Murudeshwar, then Dandeli.
- **No district booking platform.** No official Uttara Kannada tourism portal or app was found. Dakshina Kannada has one.
- **Homestays are unorganised.** Older reports count over 500 homestays in Dandeli and Joida against about 200 on record. Registration is now compulsory, new guidelines were notified in April 2026, and the High Court asked for stricter rules in June 2026.
- **Prices are confusing.** The same Dandeli rafting is quoted from ₹400 to ₹2,500, and Netrani scuba from ₹1,888 to ₹6,500, depending on the site.
- **Safety is weak.** A December 2024 report said Murudeshwar had five lifeguards for 2,000 to 3,000 daily visitors. A tour operator was booked for negligence after drownings at Gokarna in 2025.
- **Government is investing.** A January 2026 conclave signed 31 coastal projects worth ₹1,260 crore. The 2024–29 policy lists subsidies for adventure, agri-tourism, caravan parks and houseboats.

### Opportunities, ranked

| Business | Gap it fills | How it earns | Revenue potential |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homestay booking and management service | Owners lack good listings, online payment and registration help | A share of each booking plus a setup fee | High and recurring |
| Packaged group trips (corporate, college, family) | Tourists book stay, activities and transport separately | Margin on the full package. Camp packages already sell at about ₹3,500 to ₹6,500 per person per night | Highest per sale |
| Verified activity booking (scuba, rafting, kayaking, treks) | No trusted local seller with clear prices and licensed operators | Commission per ticket | Medium. Thrillophilia and others already compete |
| Travel content channel | Honnavar, Yana and Sathodi get attention but little practical local guidance | Ads and referrals | Low alone, but free marketing for the others |

### Recommendation given

Start with the homestay service, then add group packages on top of the same homestays. MakeMyTrip reportedly takes 18 to 20% from homestays, so a local service charging 10 to 15% is attractive to owners.

Example with assumed numbers (not researched figures): 25 homestays with 3 rooms each, at ₹2,500 a night, 35% occupancy and a 12% fee, gives about ₹29 lakh a year.

### Risks

- **Season:** the district banned all water sports from 1 June to 31 August 2026, so monsoon income drops.
- **Regulation:** long rafting on the Kali river is disputed with the Forest Department, and unauthorised resorts face action. Work only with licensed operators.
- **Liability:** if you sell trips, accidents can become your legal problem.

### Not verified

- Visitor numbers after 2022.
- Commission rates that local agents actually pay.
- The vehicle rental and local transport market (not researched).

## Request 2: business other than tourism

**What was asked:** "other than tourism business for outsiders". This was read as: digital businesses in Uttara Kannada that do not depend on visiting tourists, either selling the district's products to outside buyers or serving local people.

**Short answer:** the strongest one is an online brand selling the district's own foods (spices, kokum, Ishad mango products, cashew) to city buyers. These products are officially recognised but barely sold online.

### What the research showed

- **The district lives on farming.** Arecanut dominates, with pepper, cardamom, nutmeg, cashew and kokum grown alongside.
- **Arecanut income is under pressure.** Prices stayed flat for a second year and cheap imports are blamed, so growers need other income.
- **Recognised products are sold only locally.** Sirsi Supari and Ankola's Kari Ishad mango both hold GI tags, yet Ishad trade is limited to the Ankola, Karwar and Hubballi markets. The district's official "one district one product" is spices.
- **Local co-operatives are weak online.** Kadamba Marketing in Sirsi has over 40 products, but only a trade-directory listing was found, not a consumer shop.
- **Regional food brands can grow large.** Sweet Karam Coffee reached ₹46 crore revenue selling regional snacks online.
- **Advanced health care means travel.** People go to Manipal, Mangaluru or Hubballi. A ₹100 crore hospital for Karwar was only announced in the March 2026 budget.
- **Small businesses are many but tiny.** About 55,000 registered small businesses, 78% in services.

### Opportunities, ranked

| Business | Gap it fills | How it earns | Revenue potential |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online brand for district foods | Recognised products with little online reach | Product margin through your own site, Amazon and bulk orders | Highest, but needs stock and a food licence |
| Digital services for local businesses (websites, online listings, billing, marketing) | Thousands of small firms with weak online presence | Setup fee plus monthly fee | Steady, low capital, modest ceiling |
| Outsourcing team in Sirsi, Kumta or Karwar serving outside clients | Few local jobs outside farming. Officials are pushing skilling to keep youth local | Per-project or per-person billing | High if you can win clients |
| Health access service (video consultations, hospital appointment and travel booking) | No advanced care inside the district | Fee per booking | Medium. Needs medical partners, and the new hospital may reduce demand |

### What to avoid

- **Food delivery:** Swiggy and Zomato already serve Sirsi, Karwar and Bhatkal.
- **Online fish sales:** the coast is short of ice and cold storage, so this needs heavy investment.
- **Areca leaf plate export:** export orders are reported to have slowed sharply.
- **Apps for villages:** 189 villages had no mobile network in January 2026, the most in Karnataka.

### Recommendation given

If you can invest in stock, start the food brand with 5 to 8 products bought from existing processors and farmer companies, not made yourself. Focus on spices, kokum and mango products rather than supari, which carries a cancer-risk label.

If your capital is small, start with digital services. It also connects you to the producers you would need later for the food brand.

Example with assumed numbers (not researched figures): 1,000 orders a month at ₹700 gives ₹84 lakh a year in sales, and about ₹25 lakh at a 30% margin before marketing costs.

### Not verified

- The district economy figures are from 2012–13.
- Whether TSS or Kadamba already sell these foods directly to consumers online.
- Subsidy amounts for food processing in the district.
